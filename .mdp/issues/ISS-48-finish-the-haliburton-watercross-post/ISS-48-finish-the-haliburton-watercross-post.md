## Requirement

`content/posts/haliburton-watercross.md` is a `draft: true` outline.
The OWRA-sourced material is in the file. Times, prices and parking are
not, because none of them are published anywhere.

Brief: `docs/watercross_brief.md` in the site repo.

## Why it is dated

26-27 September 2026 at Pinestone Resort, the final round of the OWRA
season. The page is worth nothing on 28 September.

The slot is commercially good: it sits in the gap before the Studio Tour
weekends of 3-4 and 10-11 October, so it fills soft inventory instead of
competing with the Thanksgiving long weekend.

## What blocks it

Confirmation that the round is running. The OWRA site lists the schedule
on its homepage while its events widget says "no upcoming events" and its
registration page says "not yet open". Email
ontariowatercrossassociation@gmail.com rather than trusting the site.

The spectator section is where the search value is, and every line of it
is unresearched: start times, admission, parking, whether Pinestone runs
food service alongside, whether puddle jump runs at this round.

## Overlap with ISS-47

26 September is also the Stanhope fly-in, so two posts point at one
Saturday. That is the good case: two search intents feeding one weekend
raises the chance it fills. Cross-link them and sell the pair -- two
events on one Saturday is a stronger argument for a whole weekend than
either event alone.

Keep each title and H1 on its own event name so the two pages do not
split a generic query between them.

The real constraint is drafting time. Both this and ISS-47 want to be
live in early September and both need field verification first. If only
two of the three September posts ship, ISS-46 goes ahead of the second
of this pair, because Corduroy is the only post covering its weekend.

## Constraints

Neutral and factual unless Ben has actually been to a watercross event.
Write it as a local pointing something out, not as a memoir.

Better to write "check the OWRA page for start times" than to invent a
schedule.
