## Requirement

`content/posts/haliburton-christmas-market.md` is a `draft: true`
outline, and it is one of four possible posts out of the
November/December research.

Brief: `docs/haliburton_nov_dec_events_brief.md` in the site repo.

## Why the market went first

It is the only November or December event with a defensible 2026 date.
The tourism calendar displays Saturday 21 November, its export
parameters carry 2026 timestamps, and 21 November 2026 is a Saturday.
Everything else in the brief is undated, dated from a prior year, or not
yet announced.

Publish before mid-October so it indexes ahead of the searches. Re-date
the front matter at publish; it currently carries the day the outline
was written.

## What blocks the market post

Two things, and both are stubs in the file rather than guesses.

The hours. The calendar displays 10am to 3pm; the same listing's export
parameters work out to 9am to 2pm Eastern. A timezone bug in the
calendar plugin is the likely explanation, but it is a guess, so publish
no time until one is confirmed.

What the market actually is. The current listing has it at the arena
with no programming detail. A tourism-site event page from around
November 2024 describes something much larger, 11am to 5pm across the
arena and the Legion, with a Santa's workshop, cookie decorating,
cartoon screenings and a toy drive. There is also a second Facebook
event ID for a similarly named market. Nobody has established whether
this is one event that shrank or two different events.

The stairs-only access note needs re-verifying rather than repeating. It
is the detail most likely to strand somebody if it is wrong.

## The other three concepts -- not started

- **HIFF weekend post.** Blocked. The festival has published no 2026
  dates. Check thoseothermovies.com from early September; if nothing by
  early October, email thoseothermovieshaliburton@gmail.com. The angle
  is real: the 2025 festival's own site acknowledged the drive to the
  Kinmount opening venue.
- **November and December roundup pages.** Two pages, not one, and
  updated in place each year rather than republished. Build them in late
  October, after the tourism site refreshes its seasonal pages.
- **An early-December weekend post.** Rests on the assumption that early
  December is soft for us, which nobody has checked against the booking
  calendar. First to cut.

## Do not repeat the parade dates

The tourism site's holiday-markets page lists three parades whose
day-and-date pairs come from at least two different years. None of the
three dates works for 2026. The names, places and descriptions are
probably fine; the dates are not.

## Also outstanding

No New Year's Eve events were researched at all, and the municipal and
institutional calendars linked from the tourism site's What's Happening
page were never opened. December coverage is incomplete by construction.
