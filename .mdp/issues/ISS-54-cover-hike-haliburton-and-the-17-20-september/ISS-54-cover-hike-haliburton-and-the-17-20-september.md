## Requirement

Ben found <https://myhaliburtonhighlands.com/hike-haliburton/> on 2026-09-02
and flagged it as an autumn activity worth covering. It is worth more than
that, because of when it runs.

## The date collision

**Hike Haliburton 2026 runs 17, 18, 19 and 20 September.** The Corduroy
Enduro runs 17 to 20 September. Same four days.

The Corduroy post (ISS-46) is written, live and freshly indexed, and it
targets that exact weekend. It currently gives a reader one reason to be in
the Highlands that weekend. There are two.

That is the cheap, time-critical part of this issue, and it is the only part
that can pay off in 2026: one or two sentences on a page that already exists
and already ranks, rather than a new page with fifteen days of runway.

**Not done, deliberately.** The Corduroy post's scope was Ben's call and he
cut four things out of it. Adding to it is his call too.

## What the festival is

Run by My Haliburton Highlands, the county tourism organisation -- the same
site the November/December brief drew the christmas market from. It calls
itself Canada's largest hiking festival and dates itself to 2003, so it is
established and annual, which is what makes it worth a permanent page rather
than a one-off.

Guided hikes need registration, handled through PheedLoop. **Registration
opened 17 August 2026**, sixteen days before this issue was written.

## Do not claim availability

Anything published for 2026 must not imply a reader can still get onto a
guided hike. Registration has been open for over two weeks and nobody has
checked what is left. The festival's own system is described as capping
registrations to prevent overbooking, which means popular hikes fill.

Check before writing, or write only about the festival existing and point at
their registration page.

## The real opportunity is 2027

A post written in spring 2027 has six months of indexing runway against an
annual, established event with a searchable name. That is the same argument
the Corduroy post makes for re-cutting dates on one URL each year rather than
publishing late.

Until then the fall colours post and what is nearby (ISS-52) are the natural
homes for a link. Ben's answer on ISS-52 already establishes he is comfortable
pointing guests at outdoor activity in the area.

## Constraints

- No affiliation with the festival, no logo, no use of "official" or
  "partner". Same rule as the Corduroy post.
- Do not copy text from their site.
- Confirm the dates against the source before publishing. The tourism site has
  form for stale dates: the holiday-markets page carries parade dates from at
  least two different years.
