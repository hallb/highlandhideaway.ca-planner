## Requirement

Ben found <https://myhaliburtonhighlands.com/where-to-stay/cottage-rentals/>
on 2026-09-02, reached from their Hike Haliburton page, and asked two
questions: whether Highland Hideaway is high-end enough to sit alongside what
is there, and how a property gets listed at all.

## What the page actually holds

Ten entries. Four are rental agencies and **six are owner-managed
properties**, which answers the first question: a single owner-managed rental
is the normal shape of half that page, not the exception. One of the agencies
advertises over 120 properties, so the page is not curated for a price tier.

No prices appear anywhere on it. Each entry is a business name, a short
description, a phone number and a link out. They read as free directory
entries rather than paid advertising, though nothing on the page says so
either way.

## Why it is worth the effort

This is the third thing on the board that comes back to the same finding: a
single rental cannot outrank Airbnb or the established directories, so it wins
on local specifics and on being present where people already look. This
directory is where they already look, and it is run by the county tourism
organisation rather than by a competitor.

A link from it is also a backlink from a regional authority site on the exact
topic, which is worth more to the apex domain than anything that can be
written on it. See ISS-27 for the search work this feeds.

## How to get listed -- not published anywhere

Checked on 2026-09-02 and there is no public route:

- The cottage rentals page has no "list your business", no form, no
  membership note, no fees.
- The site's only business-facing section is
  <https://myhaliburtonhighlands.com/doing-business/>, and it is economic
  development and investment. Nothing about tourism directory listings.
- The named contact there is Scott Ovell, Director of Economic Development and
  Tourism, sovell@haliburtoncounty.ca -- the wrong desk for this.

So the route is to ask. **tourism@myhaliburtonhighlands.com**, or
1-800-461-7677 / 705-286-1777. Postal address Box 399, Minden, ON, K0M 2K0.

## This one is Ben's to send

An email to a regional tourism body under his own name, about his own
property, is not something to send on his behalf. Worth asking in the same
message whether listing costs anything and whether it requires membership of
anything, because neither is stated.

## Worth knowing before writing to them

They are the same organisation whose event calendar the November/December
research came from, and that calendar has been unreliable: hours that
contradict their own export parameters, parade dates mixing two different
years. That is a reason to check whatever they publish about the cottage,
not a reason to stay off the page.
